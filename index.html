Capacity is an envelope, not a number.

A rated capacity says what a plant can pass. The operating envelope says where it can reliably meet its treatment obligations, which constraint governs each edge, and how well each edge is known. We start with the operating record, not the design.

How we reason

The same five steps, every time.

BreakPoint does not start with the model. It starts with the physical system and the evidence. The hard part is evidence-calibrated judgement: knowing which variable matters, which calculation misleads, when literature transfers, and what evidence would actually settle the question.

Frame

Define the decision, its consequences and what would change it.

Evidence

Assemble what is known and classify every item by how it is known.

Reason

Apply water science, engineering analysis and modelling to the evidence.

Challenge

Which assumption is load-bearing? Where is absence of evidence posing as adequacy?

Decide

State the recommendation, its confidence, its conditions and the evidence that would close the gap.

Decision products

The products underneath the work.

The tools don't attempt to replace experienced judgement. They are the product of trying to capture, test and transfer what four decades of that judgement has taught us. Four decision engines apply BreakPoint reasoning to the water cycle. We use them inside our engagements today; utility and consultancy licensing follows as each product's validation record matures. Every product carries a stated evidence basis, calibration cases, known limitations, version history and change control.

Source to tap

AquaPoint

Drinking water treatment

Classifies source water, identifies the governing constraint, ranks treatment archetypes and tests candidate flowsheets across wet-season, algal, dry-season and custom scenarios.

Built, pre-release
Collection to discharge

WaterPoint

Wastewater treatment and intensification

Screens technology eligibility, reasons over full process trains, models lifecycle cost, energy and residuals, and supports rapid multi-criteria and scenario analysis as assumptions change.

Live, flagship
Residuals to resource

BioPoint

Biosolids management and transition

Evaluates eleven pathways across thirteen analysis layers, classifies PFAS capability, models the pyrolysis envelope and tests vendor claims as supported, conditional or refuted.

Built, pre-release
Effluent to reuse

PurePoint

Recycled and purified recycled water

Assesses scheme readiness against end-use class, log-reduction targets and contaminant evidence, and returns a Ready, Conditions or Not Ready verdict with the gaps to close.
