Dr Charles Taylor

Partner, UK Water and Regulatory Science

Chemist and water process scientist. Drinking water treatment, regulatory compliance, and the translation of treatment science into operational practice.

Career

Where process, water quality, regulation and operations meet.

Charles is a chemist and water process scientist specialising in drinking water treatment, regulatory compliance and the translation of treatment science into operational practice. His career spans source-to-tap water quality science at Southern Water, operational technical leadership and process engineering at Thames Water, process discipline leadership for South East Water, and independent regulatory and treatment consultancy through Scienqua.

His work sits at the point where process performance, water quality, regulation and operational reality meet. He has investigated water quality failures, led responses to treatment incidents, reviewed treatment design standards, developed and appraised capital process solutions, and, through Scienqua, is on the Drinking Water Inspectorate’s List of Consultants for Regulation 31.

He trained as a chemist at Southampton and Liverpool and took a PhD in chemistry at Warwick, working across biological chemistry and environmental microbiology, before moving into the water industry. At Southern Water he monitored and improved drinking water quality from source to tap across surface and groundwater works, investigated failures, prepared regulatory reports and trained junior colleagues in process science. At Thames Water he sat inside an operational management team as technical specialist, managing supply incidents and leading the operational and technical response to reactive treatment problems, then led the water process engineering team responsible for outline design of treatment assets and for the company’s process design standards. From 2023 to 2025 he was process engineering discipline lead for South East Water through Scienqua.

Through Scienqua he also works with international organisations to bring new treatment processes and analytical methods into the UK, including biological denitrification. At BreakPoint he leads UK delivery, the regulatory readiness test in Technology Assessment, and UK regulatory calibration of the decision products: the question of whether encoded treatment reasoning transfers into the UK regulatory and utility environment.

Selected work

Representative projects.

A selection from utility and independent work. Delivered in previous roles or through Scienqua; not represented as BreakPoint Insight deliveries.

Biological denitrification into the UK potable market

South East Water. Lead process engineer, 2024 to 2025; then Scienqua with Ingeobras, 2025

Optioneering through outline design with OPEX and CAPEX review identified biological denitrification for nitrate removal and secured executive approval to adopt it on several works. Coordinated a small-scale pilot on a groundwater works and the Regulation 31(4)(c) application to the Drinking Water Inspectorate for live testing, leading to construction of the UK’s first live biological denitrification plant.

Treatment capability and supply resilience, 40 ML/d surface water works

Thames Water. Technical specialist, 2020 to 2021

New procedures for operation and control of front-end chemical dosing during poor raw water quality at a works that had previously lost supply after heavy rainfall. Outages ceased, treatment capability and resilience improved, and the capital investment requirement was re-evaluated.

Capital refurbishment and enhanced throughput, 30 ML/d surface water works

South East Water. Lead process engineer, 2023 to 2025

Technical direction and design review of every process element of the existing stream and an additional stream to raise performance and throughput, including pre-oxidation, coagulation and manganese removal optioneering.

Chromium VI analysis and treatment need

South East Water. Lead process engineer, 2023

Review of analytical techniques and collaboration with an instrumentation supplier to quantify chromium VI accurately at low levels in groundwater, so that the need for a costly removal process could be assessed on evidence rather than assumed.

Water quality and compliance at a large north London production plant

Thames Water. Water process engineering team lead, 2021 to 2023

A series of multidisciplinary projects addressing the plant’s water quality and compliance challenges, producing a prioritised delivery mechanism for upgrades and enhancements to key processes including slow sand filtration.

Process design standards review

Thames Water. 2021 to 2023

Review of the company’s water treatment process design standards, leading to enhanced standards for filtration and for the design of sampling facilities.
